# Bayesian Reasoning Sources

Reviewed 2026-05-26.

## Primary Sources

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Bayes' Theorem".
  - URL: `https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/bayes-theorem/`
  - Used for the theorem-level relationship between hypothesis, evidence, conditional probability, and Bayesian confirmation.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Bayesian Epistemology".
  - URL: `https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology-bayesian/`
  - Used for the broader framing of Bayesianism as formal belief updating and rational credence.
- Amos Tversky and Daniel Kahneman, "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases", Science, 1974.
  - URL: `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17835457/`
  - DOI: `10.1126/science.185.4157.1124`
  - Used for the base-rate neglect and heuristics-and-biases risk that motivates explicit priors.

## Skill Interpretation

This skill paraphrases Bayesian reasoning into an agent-usable workflow. It avoids copying source text. The operational additions are:

- use priors and base rates before reacting to evidence,
- compare how expected evidence is under competing hypotheses,
- distinguish likelihood from posterior probability,
- use natural frequencies for rare-event diagnosis,
- treat correlated evidence as one evidence cluster unless independence is justified,
- report posterior confidence and residual uncertainty,
- prefer qualitative confidence bands over fake precision when inputs are weak.

## Boundary Notes

- Bayesian reasoning updates belief strength.
- Expected value uses probabilities and payoffs to choose among options.
- Prioritization ranks work items or options by task-specific criteria.
- Playing to Win formulates integrated strategy choices.
- Porter's Five Forces diagnoses industry structure.
- Statistical modeling estimates relationships or parameters from data; Bayesian reasoning can inform modeling but is not the full modeling workflow.
