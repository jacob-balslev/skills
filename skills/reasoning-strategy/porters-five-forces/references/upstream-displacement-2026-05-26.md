## Finding

No upstream displacement found.

Recent platform releases improve agent harnesses, sandbox execution, memory, skills loading, diff review, reasoning streams, OAuth/runtime behavior, and developer workflow mechanics. None replaces the explicit Five Forces method: defining an industry boundary, diagnosing entrant threat, supplier power, buyer power, substitutes, and rivalry, then translating those forces into profit-pool and positioning implications.

## Recommendation

Create and keep the skill. Treat stronger native reasoning models and agent harnesses as better hosts for the method, not as substitutes for teaching the method.
