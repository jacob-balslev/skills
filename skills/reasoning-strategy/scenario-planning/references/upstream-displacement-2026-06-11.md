# Upstream Displacement Check: Scenario Planning

Checked: 2026-06-11T08:27:54Z

## Question

Has scenario planning been displaced by a newer native model capability, AI research feature, forecasting tool, or strategy framework such that a dedicated skill should be deprecated, folded, or reframed as a small delta?

## Finding

No displacement found. Scenario planning remains a distinct strategic-foresight method. Current AI tools can help collect signals, draft scenarios, summarize sources, or run workshop facilitation, but they do not remove the need for the method's decision discipline: focal question, critical uncertainties, internally consistent alternative futures, stress-testing, signposts, and robust/contingent action design.

## Evidence Checked

- UK Government Office for Science still publishes and updates the Futures Toolkit as a practical set of tools for robust policies and strategies under uncertainty, including scenarios and policy stress-testing.
- MIT Sloan Management Review continues to host Schoemaker's scenario-planning article as a strategy reference.
- Shell continues to publish scenario materials as a practitioner foresight tradition.
- OECD continues to frame strategic foresight as an institutional capability.
- Recent scenario-planning literature discusses traps and paradoxes rather than displacement by a replacement method.

## Consequence For The Skill

Keep `scenario-planning` as a standalone reasoning-strategy skill. Its current boundary should explicitly prevent two common displacement mistakes:

1. Treating AI-generated scenario text as evidence or as a complete method.
2. Replacing deep-uncertainty work with expected-value math when probabilities are not defensible.
