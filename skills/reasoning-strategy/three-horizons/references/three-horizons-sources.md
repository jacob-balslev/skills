## Authoring Interpretation

The skill treats Three Horizons as a concurrent innovation and growth portfolio method. The operating sequence is:

1. Define the portfolio boundary, strategic ambition, decision, evidence, and privacy boundary.
2. Classify initiatives by strategic role, maturity, uncertainty, evidence, resources, governance, and metrics.
3. Separate Horizon 1 current core, Horizon 2 emerging growth, and Horizon 3 future options.
4. Diagnose portfolio balance, starvation, gap, governance mismatch, metrics mismatch, and transition risk.
5. Convert the diagnosis into rebalance actions: protect, extend, invest, incubate, partner/acquire, pause, kill, or reclassify.

The skill explicitly rejects the common "now, later, much later" simplification. Time matters, but it is not the only classification axis. A real Three Horizons analysis uses different evidence standards, governance, funding, and metrics for each horizon while managing all three now.

## Boundary Notes

- BCG Matrix owns growth-share portfolio allocation across business units or products.
- Ansoff Matrix owns product-market growth path selection.
- Blue Ocean Strategy owns value-curve redesign and market-boundary reconstruction.
- OKRs own execution goal-setting and tracking.
- Expected Value owns probability-weighted quantitative option comparison.
- Playing to Win owns integrated strategy-choice formulation before portfolio allocation.
- International Futures Forum Three Horizons owns futures and systems-change facilitation when the user explicitly asks for that variant.
