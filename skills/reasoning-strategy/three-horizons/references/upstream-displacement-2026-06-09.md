## Finding

No upstream displacement found for a Three Horizons innovation portfolio skill.

Three Horizons is a stable strategy and innovation-portfolio framework, not a vendor-specific workflow, technical workaround, agent-runtime behavior, or platform feature that could be replaced by a recent OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenCode, or open-source release. Current AI tools can help classify initiatives or summarize portfolio evidence, but they do not replace the strategic judgment: balancing the current core, emerging growth, and future options with horizon-appropriate governance and metrics.

The main boundary caveat is method ambiguity. McKinsey's Three Horizons of Growth is the business-portfolio framework this skill teaches. International Futures Forum's Three Horizons method is a related futures and systems-change facilitation frame. The skill should route business innovation portfolio tasks here, while naming the IFF variant only when the user asks for futures, system transition, or social-change facilitation.

## Recommendation

Create and keep the skill. Teach Three Horizons as a concurrent innovation and growth portfolio method, include a boundary note for IFF-style futures facilitation, and prevent the main failure modes: treating horizons as a sequential calendar, underfunding Horizon 2, using mature-core metrics on Horizon 3, and labeling exploratory ideas without learning or kill criteria.
