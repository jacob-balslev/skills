## Skill Interpretation

This skill paraphrases the framework into an agent-usable workflow. It avoids copying the book, webinar, or transcript. The operational additions are agent-facing checks:

- distinguish strategy from planning and generic goals,
- make `where to play` concrete enough to exclude alternatives,
- make `how to win` mechanism-level and falsifiable,
- tie capabilities to the advantage,
- convert management systems into owners, metrics, cadence, decision rights, and kill/pivot triggers,
- run fit checks and a reverse test before presenting the cascade as coherent.

## Boundary Notes

- Playing to Win formulates integrated strategy choices.
- Five Forces diagnoses industry structure and attractiveness.
- Seven Powers classifies durable sources of power or moat.
- OKRs translate a chosen strategy into execution objectives and measurable results.
- Prioritization ranks work items or options after the strategic frame is clear.
- Methodology designs rigorous processes and verification gates; it does not own the competitive choice cascade.
