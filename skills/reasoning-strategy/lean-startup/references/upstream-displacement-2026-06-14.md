# Upstream Displacement Check — Lean Startup

Checked 2026-06-14.

## Result

No displacement found.

Modern AI prototyping tools, analytics platforms, no-code builders, product-discovery tools, A/B testing systems, and AI research assistants can reduce the cost of building, measuring, and source discovery. They do not replace the Lean Startup methodology's core decision discipline: name the risky assumption, choose the smallest ethical test, define the metric and threshold before building, run the experiment, and make a pivot/persevere/stop decision from evidence.

## Why The Skill Remains Useful

- Faster builders can increase waste if they make teams build more before naming the learning question.
- Analytics tools can increase false confidence if teams track vanity metrics or choose success criteria after seeing results.
- AI research summaries can help find sources, but they do not validate customer behavior and should not be treated as evidence without source checks.
- Product experimentation platforms implement parts of measurement, but the operator still owns hypothesis quality, cohort design, metric choice, threshold choice, ethics, and decision interpretation.

## Follow-Up Trigger

Re-check if a mainstream product experimentation or startup-validation platform ships a first-party workflow that enforces the full assumption-to-experiment-to-threshold-to-decision loop with auditable learning milestones. If that happens, reframe this skill toward how to use that platform without losing Lean Startup's evidence discipline, rather than deleting the methodology.
