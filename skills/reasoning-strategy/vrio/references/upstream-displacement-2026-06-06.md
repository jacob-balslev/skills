## Finding

No upstream displacement found.

VRIO is a stable business strategy framework, not a technical workflow, API workaround, vendor-specific platform integration, or agent-runtime behavior that could be superseded by a recent first-party release. Current AI tools can help execute VRIO analysis, but they do not replace the conceptual skill: defining a resource, testing value, rarity, imitation cost, and organization, and mapping the result to a competitive implication.

The relevant drift risk is conceptual misuse rather than platform displacement: agents may blur VRIO with SWOT/TOWS, Seven Powers, Five Forces, BCG, Ansoff, or generic "moat" language. The new skill addresses that through explicit boundaries, sequential tests, evals, and source notes.

## Recommendation

Create and keep the skill. Treat current AI platforms as improved execution environments for VRIO analysis, not as substitutes for teaching the framework in a portable agent skill.
