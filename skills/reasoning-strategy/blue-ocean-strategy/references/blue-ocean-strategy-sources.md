## Skill Interpretation

This skill paraphrases Blue Ocean Strategy into an agent-usable workflow. It does not copy the book or official tool pages. The operational additions are agent-facing checks:

- define the current category and accepted factors of competition before proposing a new curve,
- use the strategy canvas to expose similarity and overinvestment,
- search across six boundary-reconstruction paths,
- consider all three tiers of noncustomers,
- use the Four Actions Framework and ERRC grid to produce a focused and divergent value curve,
- test buyer utility, strategic price, target cost, and adoption hurdles before calling a move value innovation.

## Boundary Notes

- Blue Ocean Strategy designs value innovation and new market space.
- Porter's Five Forces diagnoses industry structure and profit-pool pressure.
- Seven Powers diagnoses durable firm-level power or moat mechanisms.
- Playing to Win formulates integrated choices across aspiration, where to play, how to win, capabilities, and systems.
- Framework-fit-analysis chooses which method should be applied; it does not apply Blue Ocean Strategy itself.
- Prioritization ranks known work items after the strategic frame is clear.
