## Finding

No upstream displacement found.

Recent OpenAI, Anthropic, Claude Code, and OpenCode releases improve coding agents, computer use, visual/chart generation, app/desktop workflows, background agents, skills/plugin surfaces, and tool/runtime integrations. None replaces the negotiation method itself: separating people from the problem, focusing on interests, inventing mutual-gain options, using objective criteria, defining BATNA/reservation value/ZOPA, and deciding whether to accept or walk away.

The Harvard Program on Negotiation continues to present principled negotiation and BATNA as live negotiation concepts in 2026 materials. That is source currency for the method, not displacement.

## Recommendation

Create and keep the skill. Treat stronger native reasoning models as better hosts for applying the method, not as substitutes for teaching principled negotiation in a portable agent skill.
