## Skill Interpretation

This skill paraphrases the framework into an agent-usable workflow. It does not copy the book text. The operational additions are agent-facing checks:

- scope the business and arena before diagnosing power,
- name the exact candidate power,
- test benefit and barrier separately,
- distinguish proven power from plausible candidate, weak edge, and false positive,
- separate firm-level power from industry attractiveness,
- check timing and company phase,
- end with strategy implications and evidence requests.

## Boundary Notes

- Seven Powers diagnoses durable firm-level power or moat mechanisms.
- Porter's Five Forces diagnoses industry structure and profit-pool pressure.
- Playing to Win formulates integrated strategy choices.
- Blue Ocean Strategy designs value innovation and differentiated market space.
- VRIO tests whether resources and capabilities are valuable, rare, inimitable, and organized.
- SWOT/TOWS inventories internal/external factors and converts them into option logic.
